The Business Case

What Godrej actually gets

Eight ways this becomes more than a CSR line-item. Tap a card to open it.

Mile 01+

A lasting brand legacy

Not a bench, not a building — Godrej becomes known for India's first Compassion-Driven Campus Pet Community Hub. Every student, faculty member and recruiter who walks past associates the space with Godrej.

Mile 02+

Reinforces ESG commitments

A tangible, reportable example covering animal welfare, biodiversity, sustainable infrastructure, community engagement and student well-being — all in one initiative.

Mile 03+

Strengthens employer branding

Today's MBA students are tomorrow's managers and consultants. A project that visibly improves their daily campus life builds association with empathy, innovation and purpose-driven leadership.

Mile 04+

A living design showcase

Modular construction, sustainable materials, solar power, smart lighting, rainwater harvesting and low-maintenance landscaping — demonstrated in daily use, not a brochure.

Mile 05+

Built-in storytelling

"Godrej creates India's first MBA campus pet hub." Volunteer stories, adoption successes, exam-season wellness moments — natural content for social, CSR reports and annual reviews.

Mile 06+

Real employee engagement

Godrej employees can volunteer at adoption drives, join wellness events, mentor student volunteers, and take part in campus CSR — extending impact well beyond a cheque.

Mile 07+

A research collaboration

SIBM can study employee well-being, the effect of pet interaction on stress, CSR impact and sustainable campus design — giving Godrej access to fresh case studies.

Mile 08+

A scalable CSR model

If it works at SIBM Pune, the same model travels — to other universities, corporate campuses, tech parks and residential townships. One pilot, many repeats.
